# AI Customer Support Evaluation Rubric

## 1. Purpose

This rubric defines how AI-generated customer support responses will be evaluated.

The evaluation focuses on factual accuracy, compliance with the provided policy, completeness, clarity, and professional communication.

## 2. Evaluation Criteria

Each criterion is scored from 1 to 5.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Very poor — serious errors |
| 2 | Poor — several important problems |
| 3 | Acceptable — some improvements needed |
| 4 | Good — minor issues only |
| 5 | Excellent — accurate and ready to send |

### Criterion 1: Factual Accuracy

Does the response contain only information supported by the reference policy?

- 5 — All statements are accurate.
- 3 — Minor inaccuracies or unclear statements.
- 1 — Important information is invented or incorrect.

### Criterion 2: Policy Compliance

Does the response follow the provided company policy?

- 5 — Fully complies with the policy.
- 3 — Some details are missing or imprecise.
- 1 — Contradicts the policy or invents conditions.

### Criterion 3: Completeness

Does the response answer the customer's question and explain the next steps?

- 5 — Fully answers the question.
- 3 — Partially answers the question.
- 1 — Does not resolve the customer's main concern.

### Criterion 4: Clarity

Is the response easy to understand?

- 5 — Clear, logical, and concise.
- 3 — Understandable but confusing in places.
- 1 — Difficult to understand.

### Criterion 5: Professional Tone

Is the response polite, respectful, and appropriate for customer support?

- 5 — Professional and empathetic.
- 3 — Neutral but could be improved.
- 1 — Impolite, dismissive, or inappropriate.

## 3. Critical Errors

The following errors must be documented separately:

- Inventing a return period or return conditions not stated in the reference policy.
- Claiming that original packaging is mandatory when the policy does not say so.
- Promising an immediate refund without policy support.
- Giving an unsupported refund-processing timeline.
- Ignoring required approval or item-condition verification.
- Failing to explain when information is unavailable in the policy.

## 4. Communication Requirements

For Spanish customer support responses:

- Address the customer using formal **Usted**.
- Use polite and professional language.
- Explain missing information honestly.
- Avoid unsupported promises and vague timelines.

## 5. Evaluation Result

The final quality score is the average of the five criteria.

Critical errors must also be recorded, even when the average score is high.

A high score does not automatically mean that a response is safe to send if it contains a critical policy violation.

## 6. Important Note

The evaluation must always use the reference policy supplied for the test case. These criteria do not replace the actual policy of any company.
